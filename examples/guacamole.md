# Guacamole

A delicious and creamy avocado dip, perfect for parties or as a side dish.

*mexican, dip, vegan*

**4 Servings, 200g**

---

- *2* ripe avocados
- *1/4* red onion, finely diced
- *.5 teaspoon* salt
- *1 1/2 pinches* red pepper flakes
- *1* lime, juiced
- fresh cilantro

---

1. Cut avocados in half, remove the pit, and scoop out the flesh into a bowl.
2. Mash avocados with a fork to desired consistency.
3. Add diced onion, salt, red pepper flakes, and lime juice.
4. Mix well and taste. Adjust seasoning as needed.
5. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve immediately.
