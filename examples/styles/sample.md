# Shakshuka with Herbed Yogurt

Eggs gently poached in a smoky, spiced tomato and pepper sauce. A one-pan
breakfast that works just as well for a **weeknight dinner**.

*North African, vegetarian, one-pan, brunch*

**4 servings, 1 large skillet**

---

## Sauce

- *3 tbsp* olive oil
- *1* yellow onion, finely diced
- *2* red bell peppers, sliced into strips
- *4 cloves* garlic, minced
- *800 g* whole peeled tomatoes, crushed by hand

### Spices

- *2 tsp* ground cumin
- *1 ½ tsp* smoked paprika
- *¼ tsp* cayenne pepper
- *1 tsp* fine sea salt

## Eggs & toppings

- *6* large eggs
- *60 g* feta, crumbled
- *1 handful* fresh parsley and cilantro, chopped
- [Herbed yogurt](https://recipemd.org/)
- crusty bread, for serving

---

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and
   peppers and cook, stirring occasionally, until soft and just beginning to
   colour, about **10 minutes**.
2. Stir in the garlic and all the spices. Cook for one minute until fragrant.
3. Pour in the tomatoes, bring to a simmer and cook for 10–15 minutes until
   the sauce has thickened. Taste and adjust the salt.
4. Make six wells in the sauce with the back of a spoon and crack an egg into
   each. Cover and cook for 5–8 minutes, until the whites are set but the
   yolks are still runny.
5. Scatter over the feta and herbs. Serve straight from the pan with the
   yogurt and plenty of bread.

> **Tip:** Make the sauce a day ahead — the flavour only improves. Reheat
> gently before adding the eggs.
